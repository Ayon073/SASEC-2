01 / Selected work

One corridor.
A wider connection.

A project overview drawn from the supplied SASEC-2 brief, documenting a major highway upgrade in Bangladesh.

Bangladesh · Transport infrastructure

Elenga–Hatikamrul–Rangpur, upgraded.

The SASEC Road Connectivity Project-2 upgrades 190.4 km of highway to a divided four-lane route, with slow-moving vehicle traffic lanes on both sides. The program also includes the Hatikamrul interchange and supporting road-sector facilities.

02 / The approach

Infrastructure is a team effort.

Better connections come from more than pavement. They depend on sound planning, coordinated delivery, road safety, and infrastructure that works for the people who use it.

The SASEC-2 program brings those needs together along a strategic north–south corridor, linking highway improvements with a new interchange and road-sector facilities.
